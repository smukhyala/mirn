# Archive

**Nothing in this directory governs current work.**

These are the documents from MIRN's first life as a research measurement instrument for
robot-induced perturbation of pedestrian motion. They are kept for three reasons and no others:

1. **Provenance.** They are why the teaching pages are the ones they are. The five-part argument
   the site makes — the paired design, the detection floor, the confounded estimator, the placebo
   check — came from this assessment.
2. **Citation backing.** The notes take a small number of claims about the literature from here and
   present them to readers who cannot check them. Those claims cite their primary sources directly;
   this is the record of where they were found.
3. **The record that the deletions were informed rather than ignorant.** A fair amount was dropped
   in the pivot. This is what was dropped.

`mirn-research-wayfinder.md` carries a number of `UNVERIFIED` markers. They are not to be deleted, softened,
or quietly updated to match something we now believe — see guardrail 13 in `CLAUDE.md`. Its §17
records that several "no paper does this" negatives were established while the arXiv API was
rate-limiting, which is why the teaching copy says "we have not found" rather than "nobody has
published".

`superpowers/` holds the specs and implementation plans for the research-era build. The code they
describe is largely still in `src/mirn/`, which is now the measurement oracle rather than the
product.
